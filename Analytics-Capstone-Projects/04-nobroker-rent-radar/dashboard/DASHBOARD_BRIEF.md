# Dashboard Brief — NoBroker Rent Radar

**Audience:** NoBroker's pricing team, and indirectly tenants asking "is
this a fair price?"

## Must answer, at a glance
1. What's the typical rent for a given locality and flat type?
2. What drives price up or down (locality, size, furnishing, floor)?
3. Which current listings look mispriced relative to the model?

## Suggested components (adapt, don't just copy)
- Locality rankings by median rent / price-per-sqft.
- Predicted-vs-actual rent view.
- A view highlighting listings that deviate most from predicted price.

## Before you build
- Sketch it on paper first. Could someone find "what should a 2BHK in
  [locality] cost" in a few clicks?

## Publish
- Tableau Public (or equivalent free/public tool). Link it in your README.
