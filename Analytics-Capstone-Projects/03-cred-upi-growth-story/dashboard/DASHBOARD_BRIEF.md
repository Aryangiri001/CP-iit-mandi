# Dashboard Brief — CRED UPI Growth Story

**Audience:** CRED's strategy team, tracking the competitive landscape
monthly and deciding how to position a challenger app.

## Must answer, at a glance
1. Is the market broadening or concentrating — right now, and over the
   full window?
2. Who are the top players, and how has their share moved?
3. Where does CRED (or a challenger generally) sit, and what's the trend?

## Suggested components (adapt, don't just copy)
- Volume/value trend over time.
- Market share by app over time (top apps + long-tail bucket).
- Concentration measure trend — the single chart that answers the core question.

## Before you build
- Sketch it on paper first. Could a strategy analyst answer "broadening or
  concentrating" from this dashboard alone, without reading your memo?

## Publish
- Tableau Public (or equivalent free/public tool). Link it in your README.
