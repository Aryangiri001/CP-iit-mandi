# Dashboard Brief — Mamaearth Churn Predictor

**Audience:** the growth/CRM team, deciding who to target this campaign
cycle and with what offer.

## Must answer, at a glance
1. How many customers are in each churn-risk tier, and what's their
   combined value (revenue at stake)?
2. What distinguishes a high-risk customer from a loyal one — the RFM
   signals a campaign manager could recognise?
3. Which segment should be targeted first, given limited campaign budget?

## Suggested components (adapt, don't just copy)
- Customer base segmented by churn-risk tier (count + revenue per tier).
- RFM profile view — how recency/frequency/monetary differ by tier.
- A "who's at risk" list view or summary the CRM team could actually work
  from.

## Before you build
- Sketch it on paper first. A CRM manager should be able to say "target
  segment X with offer Y" after looking at this for 30 seconds.

- Tableau Public (or equivalent free/public tool). Link it in your README.
