# Dashboard Brief — DeHaat Mandi Price Watch

**Audience:** DeHaat's advisory team and field agents, who need to tell a
farmer when and where to sell, in a conversation, not a report.

## Must answer, at a glance
1. What's the seasonal price pattern for this commodity — which months
   typically pay more?
2. How do the 5-10 mandis compare on price and arrivals?
3. Given current conditions, what's the recommended selling window/market?

## Suggested components (adapt, don't just copy)
- Seasonal price curve, per mandi.
- Market comparison view (price level, arrivals volume).
- A clear "recommended window" callout, if your model supports one.

## Before you build
- Sketch it on paper first. Could a field agent use this on a phone screen
  while standing in front of a farmer?

## Publish
- Tableau Public (or equivalent free/public tool). Link it in your README.
